| 3 | UrbanSound8K classes aren't YOLO classes — why use it? | Bandwidth/compute results don't depend on class names. VGGSound covers the camera-labelable classes. |
| 4 | Is your federated learning real? | Two separate client processes, real MQTT broker, real bytes — on one laptop. On the Pi: one client, no averaging. |
| 5 | Why did FedAvg diverge at 1e-3 with IID data? | Warm start + fresh Adam each round → large early steps. FedProx / server momentum would be the next test. Valley explanation is a hypothesis. |
| 6 | Are 0.03 differences significant? | No. One seed. Claim is "no degradation", not equality. |
| 7 | Why is B so weak? | B is the minimal change (STFT only). §5.10.2 formula shows only a smaller representation saves bandwidth — the 1 KB feature vector in FB proves it. |
| 8 | Is privacy measured? | No — a data-flow argument. Gradient inversion [7] and Griffin–Lim [33] acknowledged; secure aggregation / DP = future work. |
| 9 | Isn't blackout exactly what modality dropout trains on? | Yes — blackout is the worst case and in-distribution. Degradation curve (fog, occlusion) = future work. |
| 10 | Fusion beat vision-only only after more training — fair? | No, stated in §5.8. The robust evidence is the audio-swap probe: 23/103 predictions changed vs 2/103 before. |
| 11 | Model freshness (12–48 h vs 5–30 min) was in your exposé — where is it? | Not measured (§6.7). Don't defend any freshness numbers. |
| 12 | What does live 0.53 mean with YOLO test labels? | Agreement with the teacher on 32 clips. Proves the pipeline works, not accuracy. |
